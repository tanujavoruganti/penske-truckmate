PENSKE TRUCK RENTAL — LOADING GUIDELINES AND WEIGHT MANAGEMENT
============================================================

UNDERSTANDING PAYLOAD VS. GROSS VEHICLE WEIGHT (GVW)
- Payload capacity is the maximum weight of cargo the truck can safely carry.
- GVW (Gross Vehicle Weight) = truck's own weight + driver + fuel + cargo.
- NEVER exceed the posted payload capacity. Overloading is illegal, unsafe, and voids coverage.
- Penske trucks have the GVW and payload ratings posted on the driver's door jamb.

PAYLOAD LIMITS BY TRUCK:
- 12 Foot Box Truck: 3,100 lb maximum payload
- 16 Foot Box Truck: 4,300 lb maximum payload
- Delivery Truck With Shelves: 3,800 lb maximum payload
- 16 and 18 Foot Cabover Truck: 7,200 lb maximum payload
- 18 to 22 Foot Step Van: 10,000 lb maximum payload
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Truck (Non-CDL): 10,000 lb maximum payload
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Truck (CDL): 17,000 lb maximum payload

LOADING SPACE BY TRUCK:
- 12 Foot Box Truck: 450 cubic feet
- 16 Foot Box Truck: 800 cubic feet
- Delivery Truck With Shelves: 800 cubic feet (note: shelves reduce usable floor space)
- 16 and 18 Foot Cabover Truck: 950 cubic feet
- 18 to 22 Foot Step Van: 1,000+ cubic feet
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Trucks: 1,700 cubic feet

PACKING EFFICIENCY
- In practice, only about 70-80% of the listed cubic footage is usable due to furniture shapes,
  corner gaps, and required walking/access space.
- Use 75% as the planning factor: e.g., a 16 ft truck's effective usable space ≈ 600 cu ft.
- Stack items safely: heavy items on the floor, lighter items on top.
- Appliances and large furniture go against the front wall of the cargo area (closest to the cab).

WEIGHT DISTRIBUTION
- Distribute weight evenly side-to-side to prevent handling issues.
- Place heaviest items low and centered front-to-back.
- Secure all cargo with straps or tie-downs (available as rental accessories at Penske locations).
- Loose items can become projectiles in sudden stops — secure everything.

RECOMMENDED LOADING ORDER:
1. Large appliances (refrigerator, washer/dryer) — against the front wall
2. Large furniture (sofas, dressers, bed frames) — standing upright where possible
3. Mattresses — placed vertically along the side wall
4. Boxes (heaviest at bottom, lightest on top)
5. Fragile items last, padded with blankets

RAMP AND LIFTGATE INFORMATION
- Most Penske box trucks have a rear loading ramp.
- Liftgate (hydraulic) is available on select 22-26 ft trucks as an upgrade (additional fee).
- Check that the ramp is fully extended and seated before loading. Never step on the ramp edge.
- Wheeled dollies are available for rental to move heavy appliances without lifting.

ITEMS REQUIRING SPECIAL HANDLING
- Pianos: Require piano dollies and experienced movers. Not covered by cargo protection if damaged
  due to improper handling.
- Safes: Very heavy for their size. Confirm the safe weight is within payload limits.
- Riding mowers: Drain fuel before loading (fire hazard). Secure all four wheels.
- Propane tanks: Must be completely empty before loading. Full tanks are a hazardous material.
- Artwork and antiques: Wrap individually. Not covered for breakage under standard cargo protection.

WEIGHT ESTIMATION TIPS
- A typical 1-bedroom apartment load weighs approximately 1,500–2,500 lbs.
- A typical 2-bedroom apartment load weighs approximately 3,000–5,000 lbs.
- A typical 3-4 bedroom house load weighs approximately 6,000–10,000 lbs.
- Add a 10-15% safety buffer to all weight estimates.
