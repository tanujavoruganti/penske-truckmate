PENSKE TRUCK RENTAL — MILEAGE POLICY
============================================================

HOW MILEAGE IS CHARGED
- Penske charges a per-mile rate on top of the daily/weekly/monthly base rate.
- Mileage is tracked by the odometer at pickup and return.
- The renter pays for every mile driven — there are no "free miles" included in standard rates.

PER-MILE RATES (ESTIMATED):
- 12 Foot Box Truck: $0.29 per mile
- 16 Foot Box Truck: $0.29 per mile
- Delivery Truck With Shelves: $0.29 per mile
- 16 and 18 Foot Cabover Truck: $0.39 per mile
- 18 to 22 Foot Step Van: $0.49 per mile
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Truck (Non-CDL): $0.49 per mile
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Truck (CDL): $0.59 per mile

ESTIMATING YOUR TOTAL MILEAGE COST
- Formula: Mileage cost = Total miles driven × Per-mile rate
- Example: 250 miles in a 22-26 ft Non-CDL truck = 250 × $0.49 = $122.50

UNLIMITED MILEAGE OPTIONS
- Penske sometimes offers unlimited mileage packages for long-distance one-way moves.
- Ask your Penske representative about current unlimited mileage promotions.
- Unlimited mileage is not available on all truck types or routes.

MINIMIZING MILEAGE COSTS
- Plan your route in advance to avoid unnecessary detours.
- For round trips, calculate the total round-trip mileage (not just one-way).
- For local moves (under 30 miles), the mileage charge will be minor compared to the daily rate.
- For long-distance moves (over 500 miles), the mileage charge becomes the dominant cost —
  compare this against fuel costs when choosing truck size.

LOCAL VS. LONG-DISTANCE COST COMPARISON EXAMPLE:
  Scenario: Moving 500 miles in a 16 ft truck (1 day)
  - Base day rate: $49.99
  - Mileage: 500 × $0.29 = $145.00
  - Fuel: 500 ÷ 12 MPG × $3.80 = $158.33
  - Environmental fee: $5.00
  - Estimated total (before insurance): $358.32

ODOMETER DISPUTE PROCESS
- The mileage at pickup is documented on your rental agreement and signed by both parties.
- If you believe there is a discrepancy, raise it at the time of pickup — not after return.
- GPS tracking data is available to Penske for dispute resolution.
