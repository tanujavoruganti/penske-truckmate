PENSKE TRUCK RENTAL — GENERAL RENTAL POLICY
============================================================

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum age: 23 years old for all truck categories.
- Valid driver's license required. License must not be expired, suspended, or revoked.
- The license must be issued by a U.S. state or Canadian province. International licenses require
  an IDP (International Driving Permit) paired with the foreign license.
- Credit or debit card required at time of rental for deposit. Cash rentals are not accepted.
- Deposit amount: typically $100–$200 held on the card at time of pickup; released after return.
- The renter must be present at pickup and is responsible for the vehicle throughout the rental.

ADDITIONAL DRIVERS
- Additional authorized drivers may be added at time of rental.
- Each additional driver must present a valid license and meet the age requirement.
- Unauthorized drivers are not covered under any protection plan.

RESERVATION AND CANCELLATION
- Reservations can be made online, by phone, or in person.
- Free cancellation if cancelled more than 24 hours before scheduled pickup.
- Cancellations within 24 hours of pickup may be subject to a one-day charge.
- No-shows forfeit the reservation and may be charged a no-show fee.

RENTAL DURATION AND EXTENSIONS
- Rental periods are based on calendar days (e.g., picking up Monday and returning Tuesday = 2 days).
- Weekly rates apply when renting 7 consecutive days.
- Monthly rates apply when renting 28+ consecutive days.
- Extensions must be requested before the original return time. Unauthorized late returns are charged
  the after-hours return fee plus the per-day rate.

GEOGRAPHIC RESTRICTIONS
- Trucks may not be driven into Mexico.
- One-way rentals (different drop-off location) are permitted for an additional one-way fee.
- Notify the rental location if crossing state lines, especially for CDL trucks.

PROHIBITED USES
- Hauling hazardous materials (flammable, explosive, corrosive, or toxic cargo) is strictly prohibited.
- Live animals and perishables are transported at the renter's own risk and are not covered by any
  protection plan.
- Off-road use is prohibited. Trucks are for paved/graded road use only.
- Towing other vehicles or trailers requires prior approval and appropriate towing equipment.
- Trucks may not be used for racing, stunts, or any illegal activity.

RETURN CONDITIONS
- The truck must be returned with the same fuel level as at pickup (full-to-full policy).
- The interior must be swept clean. Excessive debris results in a cleaning fee.
- Any damage discovered at return that was not documented at pickup is the renter's financial
  responsibility unless covered by a protection plan.
- All equipment (dollies, blankets, hand trucks) rented with the truck must be returned.

BREAKDOWN AND ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE
- 24/7 roadside assistance is available at no charge for mechanical breakdowns.
- Call the number posted inside the truck cab for roadside assistance.
- Accidents must be reported to both the rental location and local law enforcement immediately.
- Do not authorize any repairs without first contacting Penske roadside assistance.
