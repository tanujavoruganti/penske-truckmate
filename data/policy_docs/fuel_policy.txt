PENSKE TRUCK RENTAL — FUEL POLICY
============================================================

FUEL TYPE
- All Penske trucks in the light and medium category run on DIESEL fuel.
- Do NOT put gasoline (unleaded) into a diesel truck. Misfueling causes severe engine damage and
  the renter is fully responsible for all repair costs.
- Look for the green diesel nozzle at fuel stations. Many truck stops and travel centers carry diesel.

FUEL POLICY: FULL-TO-FULL
- Trucks are provided with a full tank of diesel at pickup.
- Trucks must be returned with a full tank of diesel.
- If returned with less than a full tank, Penske will charge a fuel service fee to refuel plus
  a per-gallon rate (approximately $0.50/gallon above retail) for all fuel needed to fill the tank.
- It is strongly recommended to fill up at a gas station close to the return location before returning.

FUEL TANK CAPACITIES BY TRUCK:
- 12 Foot Box Truck: 33 gallons
- 16 Foot Box Truck: 33 gallons
- Delivery Truck With Shelves: 33 gallons
- 16 and 18 Foot Cabover Truck: 30 gallons
- 18 to 22 Foot Step Van: 40 gallons
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Truck (Non-CDL): 70 gallons
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Truck (CDL): 70 gallons

ESTIMATING YOUR FUEL COSTS
- Use Miles per Gallon (MPG) and estimated mileage to project fuel costs.
- Formula: Fuel cost = (Total Miles ÷ MPG) × Diesel Price per Gallon
- Example: 300 miles in a 16 Foot Box Truck at $3.80/gal diesel:
    300 ÷ 12 MPG = 25 gallons × $3.80 = $95.00 in fuel
- Fuel costs are NOT included in the rental rate and are the renter's responsibility.
- The 22-26 ft trucks (Non-CDL and CDL) get approximately 13 MPG, making them more fuel-efficient
  per cubic foot of cargo than smaller trucks on long trips.

FUEL EFFICIENCY TIPS
- Avoid excessive idling; diesel trucks consume ~0.8 gallons per hour when idling.
- Maintain highway speeds of 55-65 mph for optimal fuel economy. Above 65 mph, fuel economy
  drops significantly.
- Plan fuel stops at truck-friendly fuel stations. Avoid low-clearance gas station canopies.
- Note: trucks have a higher roof height than standard cars — check clearance before entering
  any covered fuel station or parking structure.

TRUCK HEIGHT CLEARANCE REFERENCE:
- 12 Foot Box Truck: ~10 ft overall height
- 16 Foot Box Truck: ~11 ft overall height
- 22-26 Foot Box Trucks: ~13 ft overall height
- Always check posted clearance signs before driving under any bridge or overhang.
