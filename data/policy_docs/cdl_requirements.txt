PENSKE TRUCK RENTAL — CDL (COMMERCIAL DRIVER LICENSE) REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

WHICH TRUCKS REQUIRE A CDL?
- Any truck with a Gross Vehicle Weight (GVW) exceeding 26,000 lbs requires a Class B CDL
  (or higher) to operate legally in the United States.
- The 22-26 Foot Box Truck (CDL variant) has a GVW of 33,000 lbs and REQUIRES a valid CDL.
- All other trucks in the Penske light/medium lineup (GVW ≤ 26,000 lbs) do NOT require a CDL
  and can be operated with a standard state driver's license.

NON-CDL TRUCKS IN THIS FLEET (Standard License Sufficient):
- 12 Foot Box Truck (GVW: 9,900 lbs) — No CDL required
- 16 Foot Box Truck (GVW: 12,500 lbs) — No CDL required
- Delivery Truck With Shelves (GVW: 12,500 lbs) — No CDL required
- 16 and 18 Foot Cabover Truck (GVW: 17,500 lbs) — No CDL required
- 18 to 22 Foot Step Van (GVW: 19,500 lbs) — No CDL required
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Truck Non-CDL (GVW: 26,000 lbs) — No CDL required

CDL TRUCK IN THIS FLEET:
- 22 to 26 Foot Box Truck CDL (GVW: 33,000 lbs) — Class B CDL REQUIRED

WHAT IS A CLASS B CDL?
- A Class B Commercial Driver License is issued by your state DMV.
- It permits operation of a single vehicle with a GVWR of 26,001 lbs or more, OR towing a
  trailer with GVWR less than 10,001 lbs.
- To obtain a Class B CDL you must: pass a knowledge test, pass a skills/road test in a
  Class B vehicle, and meet medical certification requirements (DOT medical card).
- A Class A CDL also permits operation of Class B vehicles.

IMPORTANT FOR CUSTOMERS WITHOUT A CDL:
- If your cargo exceeds the 10,000 lb payload of the Non-CDL 22-26 ft truck, you have two options:
  1. Make two trips with a Non-CDL truck.
  2. Hire a CDL-licensed driver and rent the CDL variant.
- Penske does not provide CDL-licensed drivers. The renter is responsible for providing their own.
- Operating a CDL truck without a valid CDL is illegal under federal DOT regulations and voids
  all rental agreements and protection plans.

HAZMAT ENDORSEMENTS
- Transporting hazardous materials requires a HAZMAT endorsement on the CDL.
- Penske prohibits hazardous material transport on all rental trucks regardless of license status.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Always confirm your license class before reserving a CDL truck.
- If you are unsure whether your cargo requires a CDL truck, consult the weight and space calculator
  or ask a Penske representative.
