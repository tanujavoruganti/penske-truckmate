PENSKE TRUCK RENTAL — INSURANCE AND PROTECTION OPTIONS
============================================================

OVERVIEW
- Renting without any protection plan means you are personally liable for all damage to the truck
  and any third-party property or bodily injury claims.
- Your personal auto insurance may or may not extend coverage to rental trucks — check with your
  insurer BEFORE your rental. Most personal auto policies do NOT cover commercial/cargo trucks.
- Credit card rental coverage typically does NOT apply to moving trucks over a certain GVW.
- Penske offers several protection plans to limit your financial exposure.

PLAN 1: BASIC LIABILITY PROTECTION
- Cost: ~$14.00/day
- What it covers: Third-party bodily injury and property damage up to your state's minimum limits.
- What it does NOT cover: Physical damage to the Penske truck itself.
- Best for: Renters whose personal insurance or credit card already covers the truck.

PLAN 2: LIMITED DAMAGE WAIVER (LDW)
- Cost: ~$19.00/day
- What it covers: Physical damage to the Penske rental truck, with a $500 deductible.
- What it does NOT cover: Cargo inside the truck; third-party liability.
- Best for: Short local moves where the odds of a fender-bender are low but you want some peace of mind.
- Note: The LDW is voided if the truck is driven by an unauthorized driver, used off-road,
  or if the accident results from gross negligence.

PLAN 3: FULL DAMAGE WAIVER (FDW)
- Cost: ~$29.00/day
- What it covers: Physical damage to the Penske rental truck, with $0 deductible (no out-of-pocket).
- What it does NOT cover: Cargo inside the truck; third-party liability.
- Best for: Long-distance moves, first-time truck drivers, or any situation where you want
  maximum peace of mind about the truck itself.
- Recommended for moves over 100 miles.

PLAN 4: CARGO PROTECTION — BASIC
- Cost: ~$15.00/day
- Coverage limit: $15,000
- What it covers: Cargo inside the truck in the event of a covered accident or truck rollover.
- What it does NOT cover: Fragile items broken due to improper packing; perishables; cash; jewelry.
- Best for: Household moves with standard furniture and belongings worth under $15,000.

PLAN 5: CARGO PROTECTION — PREMIUM
- Cost: ~$25.00/day
- Coverage limit: $35,000
- What it covers: Cargo inside the truck in the event of a covered accident, rollover, or theft.
- What it does NOT cover: Same exclusions as Basic, but the higher limit covers more valuable loads.
- Best for: Households with electronics, antiques, artwork, or total cargo value over $15,000.

RECOMMENDED COMBINATIONS
- Local move, budget-conscious: LDW + Cargo Basic = ~$34/day
- Long-distance household move: FDW + Cargo Premium = ~$54/day
- Commercial delivery: Basic Liability + Cargo Basic = ~$29/day (check if business insurance applies)

EXCLUSIONS THAT APPLY TO ALL PLANS
- Damage from prohibited uses (off-road, towing without approval, hazmat, illegal activities).
- Damage caused by driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Damage from exceeding the truck's payload or GVW limits.
- Overhead damage from driving into structures lower than the truck's height (bridge strikes).
  Bridge strikes are a common and costly accident — always check clearances.
- Tire damage and mechanical breakdown due to renter negligence (e.g., running over debris).

FILING A CLAIM
- Report all accidents to Penske roadside assistance immediately: 1-800-526-0798.
- File a police report for any accident involving third parties or theft.
- Document damage with photos before moving or cleaning anything.
- Cooperate fully with Penske's claims team — withholding information may void coverage.
